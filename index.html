Welcome to Menopause Matters

Your supportive space for understanding perimenopause, menopause, symptoms, wellbeing, and community support.

What This Site Is About

Menopause and perimenopause affect every woman, yet most people grow up knowing very little about them. This site was created to provide clear guidance, supportive information, and a safe community for anyone experiencing symptoms or wanting to learn more.

Explore Symptoms

Hot flushes, anxiety, sleep problems, brain fog, joint pain — menopause symptoms vary widely and can be confusing. Our symptom library helps you understand what’s happening and how to manage it.

Join the Community

Menopause can feel isolating, but you’re not alone. Our community space lets you share experiences, ask questions, and support others going through similar changes.
