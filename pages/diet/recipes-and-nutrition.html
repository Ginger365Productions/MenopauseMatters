Foods That Support Hormone Balance

Nutrition plays a powerful role in managing menopause symptoms. The right foods can help with hot flushes, mood changes, sleep quality, weight management, and energy levels.

🌱 Phytoestrogen-Rich Foods

These plant compounds gently mimic oestrogen and may help reduce symptoms:

  • Flaxseed
  • Soy (tofu, tempeh, edamame)
  • Sesame seeds
  • Chickpeas & lentils

🔥 Anti-Inflammatory Foods

Helpful for joint pain, fatigue, and mood:

  • Leafy greens
  • Turmeric & ginger
  • Blueberries
  • Olive oil
  • Oily fish (salmon, sardines, mackerel)

💪 High-Protein Foods

Essential for muscle maintenance and metabolism:

  • Chicken, turkey, fish
  • Greek yoghurt
  • Eggs
  • Beans & pulses

🦴 Bone-Supporting Foods

Important as oestrogen declines:

  • Calcium-rich foods (yoghurt, fortified plant milk)
  • Vitamin D sources (eggs, oily fish)
  • Magnesium-rich foods (pumpkin seeds, almonds)
