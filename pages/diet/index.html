Diet & Wellbeing

Food, movement, sleep and self‑care can all support the body through menopause and beyond.

Food That Supports Hormone Changes

  • Plenty of vegetables, fruit and wholegrains
  • Sources of protein (beans, lentils, fish, eggs, lean meat)
  • Calcium‑rich foods to support bone health
  • Healthy fats (nuts, seeds, olive oil)

Movement and Exercise

  • Regular walking or gentle cardio for heart health
  • Strength training to support bones and muscles
  • Stretching or yoga for flexibility and stress relief

Sleep and Stress

  • Creating a calming bedtime routine
  • Reducing caffeine and alcohol, especially later in the day
  • Relaxation techniques, breathing exercises or mindfulness
