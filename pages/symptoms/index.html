Symptoms

A closer look at common menopause and perimenopause symptoms, what they mean and what might help.

Physical Symptoms

  • Hot flushes and night sweats
  • Sleep disturbance and fatigue
  • Joint and muscle aches
  • Headaches
  • Changes in weight and body shape

Emotional and Cognitive Symptoms

  • Mood changes, anxiety or low mood
  • Irritability and feeling “on edge”
  • Brain fog, memory lapses and concentration difficulties

Urogenital Symptoms

  • Vaginal dryness or discomfort during sex
  • More frequent urinary tract infections
  • Needing to pass urine more often

Symptoms can be mild or severe, short‑lived or long‑lasting. If they are affecting daily life, relationships or work, it’s worth talking to a healthcare professional—there are options that can help.
