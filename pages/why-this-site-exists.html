Why This Site Exists

Menopause is a major life transition, but many people feel unprepared, unheard or dismissed. This site aims to offer calm, clear information and a sense of support.

Closing the Information Gap

There is a lot of information about menopause, but it can be scattered, confusing or overwhelming. This site brings together key topics in one place, in language that is straightforward and respectful.

Supporting Real Lives

Menopause affects work, relationships, family life and self‑confidence. By talking openly and sharing practical ideas, we can make this stage of life less isolating and more supported.
