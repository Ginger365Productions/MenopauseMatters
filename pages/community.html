Community

Menopause can feel isolating. Community makes it easier to share experiences, ask questions and feel less alone.

Why Community Matters

Hearing from others who are going through similar changes can be reassuring and empowering. It can help normalise experiences, reduce shame and open up practical ideas for coping with symptoms.

Ways to Connect

  • Online forums or support groups
  • Local meet‑ups or wellbeing groups
  • Talking with friends, partners or colleagues
